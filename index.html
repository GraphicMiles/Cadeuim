Talk to Us

What exactly does the Relay agent do?

Relay monitors your missed calls and form submissions 24/7. When a lead comes in, it sends a personalized text, asks qualifying questions about the job, and books the appointment into your calendar — all automatically.

Do I need any technical skills to set it up?

No. You forward your missed calls to a Relay number or connect your scheduling tool. The whole setup takes under 5 minutes — no code, no developer needed.

Does it work for multiple team members?

Yes. Every team member's missed calls can route through Relay. You can set up individual agent rules or one shared workflow across your whole team.

How fast does the agent respond to leads?

Under 60 seconds for missed calls, under 30 seconds for form submissions. Relay texts leads almost instantly — well within the 5-minute window where conversion rates drop by 80%.

What if a lead needs a human?

The agent handles routine booking and qualification. If a lead has a complex question or an emergency, Relay flags it and escalates to your team immediately.

Does it work with my existing tools?

Relay integrates with Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Google Calendar, Outlook, and more. If you use a scheduling or CRM tool, Relay likely connects to it.

Can I customize the agent's messages?

Yes. You control the agent's tone, the qualifying questions, follow-up timing, and booking rules. Every message can be tailored to your trade and service area.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month with no commitment. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime — but most teams see ROI within the first week.
